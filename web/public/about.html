Household Manager

A private household organiser for one family. Not a public service.

Household Manager is a small personal web app used by two people in a single household to keep shared information in one place instead of scattered across texts and sticky notes.

What it does

  • Shows and edits events on the household's shared Google Calendar
  • Keeps shared checklists — repairs, errands, things to buy
  • Holds a shared grocery list and a weekly meal plan
  • Stores reference information: contacts, appliance manuals, medical details
  • Keeps freeform notes
  • Provides an assistant that can answer questions about the above and make changes when asked

Who can use it

Access is limited to the two members of the household it was built for. There is no sign-up, and accounts are not offered to anyone else.
